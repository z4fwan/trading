5. 💎 Hidden Gems — find overlooked winners

Gems = stocks where TA score is high but crowd attention is low. Look for score ≥ 70 + bullish RSI/MACD alignment.

6. ⚡ Signals — risk-first entries

Each signal shows LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk, confidence %, and timeframe. Trade LOW first when learning.

EXAMPLE LOW risk · 67% conf · TRENT · 1–3 days — “Supertrend bullish, RSI 55, volume normal.” → Small position, tight stop below last swing low.

Gems vs Signals

  • Gems = discovery
  • Signals = execution timing
  • Combine both for edge

11. Worked example (step-by-step)

Setup: Weekly shows TRENT BULLISH 58%. Daily agrees. Gem score 72. Signal LOW risk 65%.

Entry: ₹4296 (live price). Stop: ₹4180 (below support). Target: ₹4450 (weekly TA target).

Risk: ₹116/share. If account ₹1,00,000, risk 1% = ₹1000 → ~8 shares max.

Reward: ₹154/share × 8 = ₹1232 vs risk ₹928 → R:R ≈ 1.3:1 (acceptable if confidence high).

Position size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Entry − Stop)
