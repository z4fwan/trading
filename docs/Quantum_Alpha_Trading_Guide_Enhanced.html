2️⃣ 5‑minute pre‑trade checklist (beginner friendly)

Follow these steps before you press “Buy” or “Sell”. Each step takes only a few seconds.

  1. Open Dashboard – make sure you are logged in.
  2. Live Feed – confirm the market is open (Live Feed bar should be green).
  3. Macro Banner – if a red banner appears, pause and wait for the shock to settle.
  4. Review News – glance at the AI‑News panel; high‑impact news should be highlighted.
  5. View Predictions – note the direction (Bullish/Bearish) and confidence %.
  6. Trust Engine – if the trust score is 🟢 High you can proceed; otherwise consider a smaller size.
  7. Calculate Position Size – use the formulas in the next section.
  8. Final Decision – if everything looks green, click “Buy”. Otherwise, wait.

3️⃣ Core calculations – simple examples

3.1 Risk per trade (how much you can lose)

Risk amount = Account size × Risk % per trade
Example: $10,000 × 1% = $100 max loss per trade

3.2 Position size (how many shares/units)

Shares = Risk amount ÷ |Entry – Stop|
Example: Entry $50, Stop $48 → $2 risk per share
$100 ÷ $2 = 50 shares

3.3 Risk/Reward ratio (is the trade worth it?)

Reward = |Target – Entry|
R:R = Reward ÷ Risk
Example: Target $55, Entry $50 → Reward $5
Risk $2 → R:R = 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5 (good – aim for ≥1.5)

5️⃣ Worked example – step‑by‑step (beginner friendly)

Scenario: You see a bullish prediction for RELIANCE.

  • Live price (Entry): $2,850
  • AI Prediction: BULLISH, 68% confidence
  • Target: $2,950 (+3.5%)
  • Stop loss: $2,800 (‑1.75%)
  • Account size: $5,000, risk 1% → $50 max loss
Risk per share = $2,850 – $2,800 = $50
Shares = $50 ÷ $50 = 1 share (you would reduce size for a beginner)
Reward per share = $2,950 – $2,850 = $100
R:R = 100 ÷ 50 = 2.0 ✅ good

Because the trust score is high and the macro banner is OFF, you could place the trade with a tiny position (1‑2 shares) to get comfortable.

6️⃣ Risk rules – keep you safe

  1. Never risk more than 1–2% of your capital on a single trade.
  2. Always use a stop loss – never rely on “hope”.
  3. If News and Prediction disagree, skip the trade.
  4. If the Trust Engine shows Low confidence, cut size in half.
  5. Macro shock (red banner) → avoid new long positions or shrink size drastically.
  6. This guide does not guarantee profit – markets can gap.

🚀 How to Trade Using Our AI System

Our AI evaluates live market data, sentiment scores, and predictive confidence to suggest actionable trades. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Open the Dashboard: Look at the Live Feed Bar for the current price and the AI → Daily widget for the top setup of the day.
  2. Check the Prediction: The AI → Predictions card shows the direction (Bullish/Bearish) and a confidence percentage.
  3. Validate with Trust Engine: If the Trust Engine shows High confidence, you can consider entering the trade.
  4. Determine Entry & Stop: Use the Entry price from the Live Feed and set a stop‑loss a few points below, respecting the risk rule (max 1‑2% of capital).
  5. Size the Position: Apply the formula Shares = Risk Amount ÷ (Entry – Stop). Our guide provides a worked example you can copy.
  6. Set a Target: Aim for a risk‑reward ratio of at least 1.5 : 1. The AI → Predictions also gives a target price you can use.
  7. Monitor: Keep an eye on the Macro Shock banner. If it turns red, consider exiting or reducing size.

💡 Profit tip: Consistently applying the risk‑reward rule and never risking more than 2% per trade is the fastest path to steady growth.
