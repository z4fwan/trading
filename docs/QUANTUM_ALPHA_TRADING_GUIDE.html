3. Calculations before investing

3.1 Risk per trade (how much you can lose)

Risk amount (₹ or $) = Account size × Risk % per trade
Example: ₹10,00,000 account × 1% risk = ₹10,000 max loss per trade

3.2 Position size (how many shares)

Shares = Risk amount ÷ |Entry price − Stop loss|

Example:
Entry = ₹2,500   Stop = ₹2,450   Risk per share = ₹50
Risk amount = ₹10,000 → Shares = 10,000 ÷ 50 = 200 shares

3.3 Risk / Reward ratio (is the trade worth it?)

Reward per share = |Target price − Entry price|
Risk per share = |Entry price − Stop loss|
R:R ratio = Reward ÷ Risk

Prefer R:R ≥ 1.5 (reward at least 1.5× what you risk)

5. Macro shock & news

Orange/red banner (Tier-1 Event) = system detected war, sanctions, elite Fed/Treasury/NATO-style news, or high LLM urgency.

  • VETO tickers — often indices (NIFTY, SPY): system suggests not new aggressive longs.
  • Safe havens — gold, bonds, volatility names may be favoured in logic.
  • Action: Trade smaller, wider stops, or wait until banner is dismissed (✕).

7. Risk rules — discipline

  1. Never risk more than 1–2% of capital on one trade.
  2. Always use a stop loss from the prediction or chart — never “hope.”
  3. If News and Prediction disagree, skip the trade.
  4. If Track Record accuracy < 50% for that ticker, reduce size or avoid.
  5. AI improves over months via Supabase learning — trust trends, not one signal.
  6. This tool does not guarantee profit. Markets can gap, news can shock.
